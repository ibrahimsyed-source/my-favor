My Favor connects people who need everyday favors done with nearby Favor Pals who get them done — and get paid.

NEED A FAVOR?
• Post what you need — a pickup, a delivery, an errand, a quick task
• Get matched with a nearby Favor Pal
• Track it live on the map and pay securely in the app
• Rate your Pal when it's done

WANT TO EARN?
• Browse open favors near you and pick the ones that fit
• See exactly what you'll earn before you accept
• Get paid straight to your bank after each favor

WHY MY FAVOR
• Secure in-app payments powered by Stripe — your card details never touch our servers
• Private messaging with the other person, right in the app
• Ratings and reviews on both sides keep quality high
• Report and block tools to keep the community safe
• Delete your account and data anytime from Settings

Switch between requesting favors and being a Favor Pal anytime — one account does both.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Describe your favor and where it needs to happen
2. A nearby Favor Pal accepts and heads over
3. Follow their progress live and chat if anything comes up
4. Payment is handled in the app when the favor is done — no cash, no awkwardness

Favors are agreements between members and Pals; My Favor provides the platform. You must be 18+ to use My Favor.

Questions or feedback? Reach us at support@myfavor.app.
